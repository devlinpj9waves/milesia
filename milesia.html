Cancellation & Refund Policy

All bookings and service purchases made through Milesia Limited or its associated ventures (including Nine Waves and Reset Clann) are subject to the following terms. Cancellations made more than 7 days before the scheduled service or event date are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations made within 7 days of the service date may be eligible for a partial refund or credit at the discretion of the relevant venture. Deposits are non-refundable unless the service is cancelled by Milesia Limited or the relevant venture. In the event of cancellation by us, a full refund will be issued promptly. Refunds will be returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
